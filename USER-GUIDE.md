## 🚀 Getting Started

### Step 1: Visit NFTSol

Go to: **https://nftsolmarket.netlify.app**

### Step 2: Connect Your Wallet

1. **Click "Connect Wallet"** button (top right)
2. **Choose your wallet:**
   - Phantom (most popular)
   - Solflare
   - Ledger (hardware wallet)
   - Other Solana wallets

3. **Approve connection** in your wallet popup
4. **✅ Connected!** Your wallet address appears

### Step 3: Fund Your Wallet

You need SOL (Solana's native token) to:
- Mint NFTs (≈ 0.01 SOL per mint)
- Buy NFTs
- Pay transaction fees

**Where to get SOL:**
- Coinbase, Binance, Kraken (buy with credit card)
- Send from another wallet
- Use a Solana faucet for devnet testing

## 🎨 Creating Your First NFT

### Quick Start (5 Minutes)

1. **Click "Mint" tab** in the navigation
2. **Upload your image:**
   - Drag & drop or click to browse
   - Supported: JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP
   - Max size: 10MB

3. **Fill in details:**
   - **Name:** Your NFT's name
   - **Description:** Tell the story
   - **Attributes:** Add traits (optional)

4. **Review and Mint:**
   - Preview your NFT
   - Check mint cost (≈ 0.01 SOL)
   - Click "Mint NFT"

5. **Approve transaction** in your wallet
6. **Wait for confirmation** (~1 second)
7. **🎉 Success!** Your NFT is now on Solana!

### Pro Tips

✅ **Best Practices:**
- Use high-quality images (at least 1000x1000px)
- Write compelling descriptions
- Add meaningful attributes
- Choose unique names

✅ **Image Tips:**
- Use PNG for transparency
- Keep file size reasonable
- Test preview before minting

## 🛒 Buying NFTs

### Browse the Marketplace

1. **Click "Market" tab**
2. **Browse NFTs:**
   - Scroll through the grid
   - Click any NFT for details
   - View full-size images

3. **Filter & Search:**
   - Filter by price range
   - Search by name
   - Sort by date/price

### Purchase Process

1. **Find NFT you want**
2. **Click "Buy" button**
3. **Review details:**
   - Price in SOL
   - Current owner
   - Transaction fee

4. **Approve in wallet**
5. **Wait for confirmation**
6. **✅ You now own the NFT!**

### What You Get

- ✅ **Ownership:** Proven on Solana blockchain
- ✅ **Metadata:** Name, description, attributes
- ✅ **Image:** Stored on decentralized IPFS
- ✅ **Royalties:** Set by creator (paid automatically)

## 🎬 Eternal Echoes

### What are Eternal Echoes?

Eternal Echoes are **collaborative NFTs** where multiple creators can add layers, building on each other's work to create evolving digital art with historical context.

### Creating an Echo

1. **Click "Echo" tab**
2. **Start New Echo:**
   - Upload base image
   - Add historical context
   - Set initial story

3. **Mint First Layer**
4. **Share with community**

### Building on Echoes

1. **Browse Echoes**
2. **Choose one to build on**
3. **Add Your Layer:**
   - Upload your contribution
   - Add to the story
   - Verify historical accuracy

4. **Mint Your Layer**
5. **✅ Now part of the collaborative piece!**

### Viewing Echoes

- **See all layers** in chronological order
- **Read the story** as it evolves
- **View contributors** and their additions
- **Explore history** of each Echo

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### ❌ Wallet Won't Connect

**Solutions:**
1. Make sure wallet extension is installed
2. Refresh the page
3. Clear browser cache
4. Try different browser
5. Check wallet is unlocked

#### ❌ Transaction Fails

**Common Reasons:**
- Insufficient SOL balance
- Network congestion
- Wallet needs approval
- Transaction canceled

**Solutions:**
1. Check SOL balance
2. Wait a moment and retry
3. Approve in wallet popup
4. Check network connection

#### ❌ Image Won't Upload

**Solutions:**
1. Check file size (< 10MB)
2. Check file type (JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP)
3. Try different image
4. Check internet connection

#### ❌ NFT Not Showing

**Solutions:**
1. Wait a moment (indexing takes time)
2. Refresh the page
3. Check wallet address is correct
4. View on Solana Explorer: https://explorer.solana.com

## ❓ FAQs

### General Questions

**Q: Do I need an account?**
A: No! Just connect your Solana wallet. No email, password, or account needed.

**Q: How much does it cost to mint?**
A: Approximately 0.01 SOL (~$2-5 depending on SOL price) + 2.5% platform fee.

**Q: Can I mint for free?**
A: No, but costs are very low on Solana compared to Ethereum.

**Q: What happens to my NFTs if NFTSol shuts down?**
A: Your NFTs are on Solana blockchain - they exist forever, independent of any website.

**Q: Can I delete my NFT?**
A: No, NFTs are permanent on blockchain. But you can transfer them to burn addresses.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Which wallet should I use?**
A: Phantom is most popular, but any Solana wallet works. Ledger for maximum security.

**Q: What's the difference between devnet and mainnet?**
A: Devnet is for testing (free SOL). Mainnet is real blockchain with real SOL.

**Q: How fast are transactions?**
A: Usually < 1 second confirmation, ~13 seconds finality on Solana.

**Q: Are my NFTs stored on blockchain?**
A: Ownership is on-chain, images/metadata are on IPFS (decentralized storage).

### CLOUT Tokens

**Q: What can I do with CLOUT?**
A: Currently for reputation/display. Future: premium features, discounts, governance.

**Q: How do I earn more CLOUT?**
A: Mint NFTs, make purchases, create popular content, engage with community.

**Q: Can I transfer CLOUT?**
A: Yes, CLOUT is an SPL token and can be sent like any Solana token.

### Eternal Echoes

**Q: Who owns an Eternal Echo?**
A: The creator of each layer owns their layer. The complete Echo is collectively owned.

**Q: Can I remove my layer from an Echo?**
A: No, layers are permanent once minted. Choose carefully before adding.

**Q: How do I verify historical context?**
A: Use Internet Archive integration and cite sources when adding context.
