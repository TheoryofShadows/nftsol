# 🚀 NFTSol Launch Strategy & Go-to-Market Plan

## **📊 Product Overview**

**NFTSol** - A complete NFT marketplace platform with real Solana blockchain integration featuring:
- ✅ Real NFT minting on Solana devnet/mainnet
- ✅ SOL withdrawal system with admin controls
- ✅ Professional UI/UX with modern design
- ✅ Wallet integration (Phantom, Solflare, etc.)
- ✅ Complete admin dashboard and management
- ✅ Enterprise-grade security and monitoring

## **💰 Revenue Model**

### **Primary Revenue Streams:**
1. **Transaction Fees** - 2.5% on NFT sales
2. **Minting Fees** - Small fee for NFT creation
3. **Premium Features** - Advanced tools for creators
4. **Partnership Revenue** - Collaborations with brands

### **Pricing Strategy:**
- **Free** - Basic NFT minting and marketplace access
- **Premium** - $9.99/month for advanced features
