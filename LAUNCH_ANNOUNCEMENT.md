## **💰 How to Withdraw SOL**

### **Step 1: Connect Your Wallet**
1. Click "Connect Wallet" in the top right
2. Select your preferred wallet (Phantom, Solflare, etc.)
3. Authorize the connection

### **Step 2: Request Withdrawal**
1. Go to your dashboard
2. Click "Withdraw SOL"
3. Enter the amount you want to withdraw
4. Enter your destination wallet address
5. Click "Submit Request"

### **Step 3: Track Your Withdrawal**
1. View your withdrawal status in real-time
2. Get notified when it's approved
3. Receive transaction confirmation when completed
4. View on Solana Explorer
