QUICK FIX FOR ATA CREATION:

The fee-payer wallet (temp-keypair.json) doesn't have enough SOL.

SOLUTION:
1. Get the fee-payer address:
   solana-keygen pubkey /mnt/c/Users/KHK89/NFTSol/temp-keypair.json

2. Send 0.003 SOL from your 3XE wallet (3XEs3MJ...) to that address

3. Then create ATA:
   spl-token create-account 62hWQAgAV4jugHSuZsMqzxZNVXaVLrbRpz3Sw58Z64Mw \
     --owner 3XEs3MJ8PFiqTTqrK6RAkK9vt95jQQ1hKNNKHiE6jJ3o \
     --fee-payer /mnt/c/Users/KHK89/NFTSol/temp-keypair.json \
     --url https://api.mainnet-beta.solana.com

ALternatively, if you have 3XE wallet in Phantom:
1. Import the seed phrase into a keypair file
2. Use that as fee-payer instead
